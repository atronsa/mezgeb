## A roadmap task replaced by an earlier decision

The roadmap's first draft said:

> A late receipt after the return is booked to a suspense account plus a
> dispute case (`offline_late_receipt.v1`).

**ADR-0019 decided otherwise in step 2.10, with the maintainer.** A valid
late receipt is paid:
- by the payer's wallet first;
- then by the loss account 5030, with the shortfall recorded against the
  payer, who gets no new voucher until it is repaid.

Suspense would hold the merchant's money with no rule for releasing it.
The loss policy pays the merchant who acted in good faith, puts the cost on
the payer whose device signed, and bounds the platform's share. This step
builds the ADR's policy: `offline_receipt_pay.v1`, shortfalls and the block.
A dispute case is opened only by a conflict or an over-spend, where the
facts need a person.

## Implementation tasks

| Task | Done |
|---|---|
| `WAITING_FOR_GAP` handling: settle a contiguous chain, hold later receipts, set the gap deadline | Receipts settle in counter order; a later one waits. Settling one settles those waiting behind it (step 2.12). The deadline is 72 hours after the waiting receipt arrived (`GAP_TIMEOUT_MICROS`). At the deadline, or at the voucher's return, the waiting receipts settle past the gap, marked `gap_skipped`. A missing receipt arriving later must match its successor's `prev_digest`, or it is a `CONFLICT` |
| On a conflict: freeze the device; store both receipts; settle nothing beyond the last contiguous verified counter; raise an alert | The device and voucher freeze. The first receipt stays; the second is kept in `offline_receipt_conflicts`. Every later receipt is `FROZEN`, and the tick leaves a frozen voucher alone. An `offline.freeze` audit event is written, and a dispute opens |
| An expiry worker returns unspent escrow to the wallet | `offline_tick`, run by the service (`MEZGEB_OFFLINE_TICK_SECONDS`, 60 by default): `ACTIVE` → `GRACE` at expiry, then the return after 7 days (E18) → `RETURNED`, then `ENDED` after the late deadline (90 days by default, `mezgeb-cli offline deadline`) |
| A late receipt after the return is booked to suspense plus a dispute case | **Replaced by ADR-0019's loss policy** (above). `SETTLED_LATE`: E28, or E29 with a shortfall. After the deadline, `EXPIRED`, with nothing posted |
| `disputes` table and operator endpoints to resolve them | `offline_disputes` and `offline_dispute_payments`; list, get, resolve. **`paid`:** every valid receipt in counter order, from the escrow left (E26), then the wallet, then 5030; a conflicting copy from the wallet and 5030 only (E31, INV-21). **`refused`:** none, marked `REFUSED`. Either way the escrow left returns (E27) and the device is revoked. Idempotent; the other resolution is refused |

## Found and fixed during the step

- **A conflicting copy must not be paid from escrow.** The first version
  of `resolve_dispute` paid every receipt from escrow first. E31 and
  scenario 1 say escrow pays one counter once (INV-21), so a clone's second
  receipt is paid from the wallet and 5030 only. Fixed before the engine PR,
  and asserted in `a_disputed_clone_is_paid_from_the_wallet`.
- **A missing loss account.** In the API's contract test, a late receipt
  whose payer could not pay failed with a 500, because the test world had
  not opened 5030. That is correct (the engine never books into a missing
  account), but it is now in the runbook: open 4010 and 5030 before turning
  offline payments on.
- **A required check that never ran.** GitHub did not run the
  `conventional-title` check on #132 and #133. Editing the titles
  re-triggered it.

**The whole suite.** On the combined branch before the engine PR, `cargo
test --workspace` against PostgreSQL ran 46 test binaries and 358 tests,
with 0 failures. On the final `main` (after #139): 46 binaries, 359 tests,
0 failures.
