## Testing requirements

| Requirement | Done | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Desk-check every threat against the protocol | [threat-addendum.md](../offline/threat-addendum.md): 20 threats, each with its controls and the protocol section | 10 prevented; 9 bounded (by the voucher, the tier and per-customer caps, or the late deadline), most of them also detected; 1 detected (OT-18, through the hash chain). The open points are listed |
| Walk 10 adversarial scenarios on paper | [scenarios.md](../offline/scenarios.md): clone, replay, forged amount, clock rollback, payee double-upload, payer never syncs, lost device, partial sync, voucher expiry race, colluding merchant | Each walked step by step, with every receipt's outcome |

Walking the scenarios refined the design in three places:

- **The payee's challenge** binds a receipt to the moment of payment.
- **Digests exclude the signature,** because ECDSA can sign one body two
  ways, and two signatures of one body must not be a conflict.
- **A lost device's voucher is never closed early,** because receipts may
  still be on their way.

## Acceptance criteria

**Every scenario has a defined outcome and a defined accounting result.**
The [summary table](../offline/scenarios.md#summary) gives, for each of the
ten:

- its outcomes;
- its worked entries;
- the platform's loss.

Every protocol state has an entry (E16–E18, E25–E31), or is named as
posting nothing. Every entry balances, checked by script.

## Exit gate

**An external security review of the protocol is requested.**
[review-request.md](../offline/review-request.md) states the scope, eight
questions for a reviewer with a cryptography background, and what is asked
back.

**No reviewer is engaged yet.** As with the accounting and dispute reviews,
it needs a person. It must be done before step 2.11 builds device
registration, and before any card is personalised.
