## Exit gate

**Gate:** the invariant list is reviewed and frozen at v1. Changes go through
ADRs from now on.

The 26 invariants were reviewed in plain language on 2026-11-10, together with
three decisions to accept explicitly:

| Decision | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Safeguarding is a monitored condition (MON-1), checked daily, not an invariant checked on every posting | Accepted |
| INV-8 lets an account go negative only when it is explicitly allowed; merchant debt remains an open question for the accounting review | Accepted |
| Can a reversal itself be reversed? | **No.** Added to INV-14, with test T-14c. If a reversal was a mistake, the original is posted again as a new entry. |

**Result: passed.** The catalogue was approved and is frozen at
[v1](../invariants.md).

## Regulatory applicability

No regulatory answer is confirmed yet, and none is treated as binding. The
21 questions wait for a legal advisor. When answers arrive, they are recorded
in [regulatory.md](../regulatory.md), and any that change the design get an
ADR.
