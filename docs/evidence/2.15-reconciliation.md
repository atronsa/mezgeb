## Decisions made in this step

- **The statement format.** The partner bank's format was still an open
  research item. With the maintainer, Mezgeb reads one generic CSV layout,
  and each bank's export through a mapping file: it works with any bank and
  keeps Mezgeb independent of one. ISO 20022 camt.053 can be added as a
  second reader without changing the matching.
- **Money breaks are resolved through suspense, both ways.** The bank can
  have more than the ledger (9010, as in step 2.02) or less: for the second
  there was no account, so 9040 Suspense: unexplained bank debits is new.
  5020 Bank charges becomes active, as the cause a 9040 item most often
  closes to.
- **A line repeated with the same bank ID is not a break.** It is the same
  bank transaction printed twice and is skipped, as a re-import is.
  `duplicate_statement_line` is a line with its own ID that repeats
  another's date, amount and reference, which can be real money.

Also tested:

- **The offline reconciliation** (`crates/engine/tests/reconciliation_offline.rs`):
  a clean voucher life has no break; counters a device reported but nobody
  uploaded become a `missing_receipts` break only once the voucher has
  ended, and the break clears when the receipt arrives, even refused; a
  device that reports less than it signed is a `counter_behind` break.
- **Against the offline simulator.** Every simulation lane against
  PostgreSQL now ends with the offline reconciliation, and fails on any
  `receipt_entry_mismatch` or `escrow_mismatch`: a 20,000-operation run
  with 593 injected faults (seed 20261117), 1,567 settled receipts
  including late ones and disputes, found none.
- **Late lines clear their breaks; conflicts are explained**
  (`late_lines_clear_their_breaks_and_conflicts_are_explained`).
- **The API** (`crates/api/tests/contract/reconciliation.rs`): every
  operation called and every response checked against the contract,
  including a money break resolved and a stale cache cleared; the
  authorization matrix (63 operations × every role) and the audit test
  cover the six new operations.
- **The CLI** parses each command and prints each break on one line.
- **The worked entries** E32–E35 balance, use only chart accounts, and are
  each booked line for line by their template (`crates/ops/tests/reconciliation.rs`).

## Measurements

`mezgeb-bench --recon 1000000`, on the laptop above (release build):

| Phase | Items | Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ledger written through the engine (not reconciliation) | 500,000 lines in 19,800 entries | 1,731.8 s |
| Statement parsed | 500,100 lines | 1.8 s |
| Statement imported | 500,100 lines | 106.2 s |
| Reconciled: matched, breaks recorded, committed | 1,000,100 items | 96.5 s (10,363 items/s); recorded runtime 63.7 s |

```text
ledger: 500000 lines in 1010, 19800 entries, written in 1731.8 s
statement: 500100 lines parsed in 1.8 s, imported in 106.2 s
run: 1000100 items (500100 statement lines, 500000 ledger lines) reconciled in 96.5 s (10363 items/s); recorded runtime 63.7 s
matched 499800 · breaks: missing in statement 100, missing in ledger 100, amount mismatch 100, duplicate 100 (each 100 planted) · other breaks 0 · false matches 0
PASS: every planted break found and classified, no other break, zero false matches
```

The recorded runtime ends before the commit; the commit checks the
deferred foreign keys of half a million matched lines, which is most of the
difference. A day of a real platform is a few thousand lines, which
reconcile in well under a second.

## What it does not cover

- **A real partner bank's export.** The mapping is tested on an invented
  bank layout; the first real bank needs its mapping written and tried on
  a sample export (statement-format.md, "Adding a bank").
- **Returning unidentified money to its sender** (Dr 9010 / Cr 1010) still
  has no template (step 2.02's open question).
- **Statement balances.** A bank's opening and closing balance lines are
  not read; agreement is checked line by line. A balance check is a small
  addition if a bank's export carries them.
