## Acceptance criteria

**Fee revenue in the trial balance equals the sum of the fee lines.**

- In the worked story: after E20, 4010 in the trial balance (3.40) equals
  the fee lines posted to it, with the credits from E07–E09 and E17, less
  the fees given back in E11 and E12 (`each_template_books_its_worked_entry`).
- Under load: `fee_revenue_equals_the_sum_of_the_fee_lines` books 2,000
  random merchant payments, from 10 payers to 4 merchants, through every
  channel, with merchant-paid, payer-paid, both and no fees. 4010's balance
  equals the sum of its fee lines, which equals the sum of the fees
  requested. Each merchant is owed exactly the sum of its net amounts, and
  the reference ledger's invariants hold.

## Exit gate

**Every channel in the product list books through a template.** ADR-0014
lists the product's channels and the template that books each.
`every_channel_books_through_a_template` checks that:

- each merchant channel (`qr`, `card_tap`, `phone_tap`, `fingerprint`) books
  through `merchant_payment.v1`, with the channel recorded on the entry;
- a missing channel (2019) or any other value, such as `nfc`, `QR` or empty
  (2020), is refused;
- a payment request books through `payment_request_settle.v1`, which refuses
  one without its `payment_request` (2019) and can pay a customer or a
  merchant but never fee revenue (2010);
- the P2P transfer books through `p2p_transfer.v1` (step 2.01 tests).

## Decisions

From [ADR-0014](../adr/0014-payment-channels-and-fees.md):

- **The channel's allowed values are part of the template version.** A new
  channel is `merchant_payment.v2`, because a released version never
  changes.
- **The client computes and rounds fees.** The ledger only checks them.
- **No fees on deposits, withdrawals or returns in this version.** Step 2.02
  deferred these to this step. No product charges them, so 4020 and 5020
  stay reserved, and a future fee there is a new template version with a fee
  line.
