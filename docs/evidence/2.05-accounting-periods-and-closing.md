## Implementation tasks

| Task | Done |
|---|---|
| `periods` and `period_snapshots` tables | Yes: migration 0007. One open and at most one closing period (partial unique indexes); a period only moves open → closing → closed; snapshots are append-only |
| `close_period`: 1. block new postings dated in it | Yes: the **switch**. Postings take the period lock shared and read the open period under it; the switch takes it exclusively, waits for postings under way, records the period's last entry and opens the next period. Every later posting goes to the next period |
| 2. run verify for the period | Yes: every entry of the period lies in its range and balances in every currency, and each account's totals are computed two ways (previous snapshot plus the period's lines, and the stored running totals) and must agree. The full `mezgeb-cli verify` runs after the close in the runbook |
| 3. write snapshots, 4. mark closed | Yes, in one transaction, only if verification is clean. With findings, nothing is written and the period stays `closing`; running the close again retries it |
| A DB trigger rejecting lines whose entry's period is closed | Yes: entries and lines are refused unless their period is open when written, and entries are checked again at commit (`MZ008`) |
| Reports: `trial_balance(as_of)` and `general_ledger(account, from, to)` | Yes |

**Effective date vs posting date.** An entry belongs to the period open when
it is *posted*. Its original date is its `event_time`. A late posting, such
as an offline payment from last week, goes to the current open period with
that reference to its original date, as the step asks. The reports use the
posting date (`recorded_time`); a report by event date reads `event_time`.

## Testing requirements

| Requirement | Test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Posting into a closed period is rejected (engine and DB) | `posting_into_a_closed_period_is_refused` | Pass. The database refuses an entry written into a closed period (`MZ008`), a line added to one of its entries, reopening a period, changing a snapshot and deleting a period. The engine refuses to post with `NoOpenPeriod` when no period is open |
| Snapshot + lines after it = current balance | `a_snapshot_plus_later_lines_is_the_current_balance` | Pass, for every account, after 40 more random postings |
| Close while postings are in flight: no posting lands in the closed period | `closing_while_postings_are_in_flight` (8 writers, 20 connections, 480 postings) | Pass: five closes while the writers run, and one after. Every close is clean, no entry of any period lies after its end, all 486 entries are accounted for, and every snapshot equals the recomputation |
| The trial balance always balances | `reports_balance_and_agree_with_each_other`; every day of the 30-day run | Pass: as of now, at a period's end and at a time. The trial balance at a time before the close equals the one from the snapshot, and an unclosed period is `PeriodNotClosed`. The general ledger split at any time opens where the first half closed |

Also tested:

- **A close that stopped part-way.** After the switch, the next
  `close_period` finishes the same period and opens nothing new.
- **A tampered period stays `closing`.** A line slipped into one of the
  period's entries, around every trigger, makes verification report
  `EntryUnbalanced` and `TotalsDisagree`. Nothing is written and the period
  stays `closing`.
- **The CLI.** `mezgeb-cli close-period` was run against a migrated local
  database: it closed three periods in turn and printed each range, and an
  empty period prints "no entries".

## Acceptance criteria

**Snapshots verified against the full recomputation for 30 simulated
days.** `thirty_days_of_snapshots_match_the_full_recomputation` posts 5 to
34 random payments, transfers and deposits a day, then closes the day.
After each of the 30 closes:

- the close is clean;
- every account's snapshot equals the sum of every line up to the period's
  end, recomputed from scratch;
- the trial balance from the snapshot balances, and equals the one from the
  stored totals.

All 30 days pass.

## Cost

Every posting now takes one more advisory lock, which is shared and
uncontended, and fires two more triggers. On this machine (Windows, Docker
Desktop), two runs of `mezgeb-bench` before and after this change, 8
seconds each:

| Scenario | Before (two runs) | After (two runs) |
|---|---|---|
| 2-line, uniform accounts | 191, 243 /s | 183, 209 /s |
| 4-line, uniform accounts | 168, 206 /s | 175, 182 /s |
| 2-line, one hot account | 52, 53 /s | 51, 52 /s |

The run-to-run noise here (about 25%) is larger than any difference, so the
cost cannot be told apart from it. Every run verified clean. The
performance step (3.01) measures it on the benchmark machine.
